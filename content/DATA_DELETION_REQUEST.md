# Data Deletion Request

Effective date: April 18, 2026

This page explains how users of **Ultimate Work Hours Tracker** can request deletion of data associated with third-party services used by the app.

## Contact for deletion requests

Email: dantechstudio.sup@gmail.com

Use subject: `Data deletion request - Ultimate Work Hours Tracker`

## What to include in your request

To help us process your request, include:

- app name: `Ultimate Work Hours Tracker`;
- platform: Android or iOS;
- app variant if known (production or development);
- approximate date of use/purchase;
- RevenueCat App User ID (if available from app settings/support flow);
- Google Play order ID or Apple transaction reference (if relevant to a purchase).

Do not send sensitive documents unless explicitly requested.

## What data can be deleted

We can process deletion requests related to data under our control and data processed through providers we use for app operation:

- Firebase Analytics-related app usage records linked to app-level identifiers (where technically supported by provider tooling);
- RevenueCat customer profile/subscription metadata linked to provided identifiers (where technically supported by provider tooling);
- support request records you sent to us by email.
- optional location geofence settings stored by the app on your device (for example location name, coordinates, and radius) can be removed by deleting/editing jobs or clearing app storage.

## Data that users can delete directly on device

Most work-hour data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it without contacting us by:

- deleting/editing shifts in the app;
- clearing app storage in device settings;
- uninstalling the app;
- deleting exported files from where you saved/shared them.
- disabling location auto punch and removing saved work locations in the app.

## Data that may be retained

Some records may be retained where required for legal, fraud-prevention, billing, tax, accounting, or security reasons, or where deletion is controlled by app store/platform providers.

Examples:

- Google Play / Apple transaction records maintained by store providers;
- records required by law or contractual obligations.

## Expected timeline

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 days and complete processing within 30 days where feasible. Complex requests may take longer depending on provider constraints.
