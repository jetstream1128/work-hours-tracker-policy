Data Deletion Request

Effective date: April 18, 2026

How to request deletion

Send your request to:

dantechstudio.sup@gmail.com

Suggested subject: Data deletion request - Ultimate Work Hours Tracker

What to include

  • App name and platform (Android/iOS).
  • Approximate date of use/purchase.
  • RevenueCat App User ID (if available).
  • Google Play order ID / Apple transaction reference (if applicable).

Data we can delete

  • Support emails/records you sent to us.
  • RevenueCat customer/subscription metadata linked to your identifiers (subject to provider capabilities).
  • Firebase Analytics-related app usage data linked to request identifiers (subject to provider capabilities).
  • Optional location geofence job settings stored on device can be removed in-app or by clearing app storage.

Data you can delete directly

  • Delete or edit shifts inside the app.
  • Clear app storage on your device.
  • Uninstall the app.
  • Delete exported files from where you saved/shared them.

Data that may be retained

Some records may be retained for legal, security, anti-fraud, tax, accounting, or billing reasons, or where retention is controlled by app store/platform providers.

Timeline

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 days and complete processing within 30 days where feasible.
