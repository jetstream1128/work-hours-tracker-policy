Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Ultimate Work Hours Tracker and explains how we handle information when you use the app.

1. Contact

Developer: Bohdan Alieksieiev

Email: dantechstudio.sup@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • The app is designed to work primarily on your device.
  • The app does not require account creation for core use.
  • Shift history and settings are stored locally on-device.
  • Exports (for example PDF/XLSX) are created only when you request them.

3. Data processing and storage

Depending on your usage, the app may process shift records, app settings, local backups, and locally generated export files. In the current version, this data is intended to stay on your device unless you explicitly share exported files.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Data may leave the app only through actions initiated by you (for example using OS share features), or through platform-level processing controlled by your device/app store.

5. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until edited/deleted by you, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. You can also remove exported files from the location where you saved them.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time and will update the effective date.
