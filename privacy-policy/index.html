Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Ultimate Work Hours Tracker and explains how DanTechStudio handles information when you use the app.

Contact

Developer: Bohdan Alieksieiev

Email: dantechstudio.sup@gmail.com

What data is processed

Local on-device data

  • Shift records, settings, local backups, and exported files you create.
  • Notification/widget configuration needed for app functionality.

Third-party service processing

  • Firebase Analytics for product usage events and app-level analytics properties.
  • Firebase Remote Config for runtime app configuration values.
  • RevenueCat and app stores for purchase/subscription status handling.

How data is used

  • Run core shift-tracking and payroll estimate features.
  • Save settings and support reminders/widget behavior.
  • Generate and share exports when requested by you.
  • Improve app quality and support purchases/restoration.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data.

Data may be shared with service providers used by the app (Firebase, RevenueCat, platform stores), and with destinations you choose when sharing exported files.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data remains until edited/deleted by you, app storage is cleared, or uninstall.
  • Automatic backups are kept in a limited on-device rolling set.
  • Third-party retention follows provider policies.
